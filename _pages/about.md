Hi, I'm Rupom. I'm a graduate engineer with a Bachelor of Engineering in Mechatronics (with Honours) from the University of Newcastle, and I'm looking for my first role in the Australian engineering industry.


My background sits where mechanical, electrical and software engineering meet. I enjoy PLC programming, HMI design, control loop tuning, and building embedded and robotic systems. I also work with simulation and modelling in MATLAB, and write code in Python and C++. I have done an elective with html programming and I am also using it to arrange this portfolio website.


Before finishing my degree I worked an internship as an Assistant Engineer at Nortex Textile Mills in Bangladesh. There I monitored factory sensors, helped build automated maintenance processes that use machine learning to predict and prevent production downtime, and worked with upgrade plans to the plant's fire safety systems.


Outside of engineering, I've volunteered at University of Newcastle events and raised funds with the JAAGO Foundation to build and renovate schools in rural Bangladesh. I was part of the UoN Dance club. I speak English, Bengali, Hindi and Urdu, and I'm learning Japanese and AUSLAN.
